    FAQ

    Common Canada travel restriction questions

    Is this an official Canada travel restrictions tool?

    No. It is a practical checklist that points you to official Canada, CDC, and U.S. travel sources.

    Why does the tool ask about DRC, Uganda, and South Sudan?

    Current U.S. Ebola-related travel measures mention recent presence in those countries. This matters most when your final destination is the United States or your itinerary is handled by a U.S.-bound carrier.

    What should I do if I travel today?

    Check official sources, call the airline, and confirm your entry document before leaving for the airport or border.
